# Kumiki Blender: beginner's guide

**Never opened Blender? Never made a 3D model? Start here.** You do not need to know Python or 3D modeling to follow the first lesson.

Kumiki Blender is a toolkit that builds 3D objects from instructions. You run a command, it creates a model in Blender, and you can inspect or edit the result. It is primarily a **command-line tool**, not a point-and-click Blender add-on.

## Your first journey

1. [Start Here](https://github.com/YukiUmetsu/kumiki-blender/wiki/Start-Here): understand what you are making (5 minutes).
2. Install on [Mac](https://github.com/YukiUmetsu/kumiki-blender/wiki/Install-on-macOS), [Windows](https://github.com/YukiUmetsu/kumiki-blender/wiki/Install-on-Windows), or [Linux](https://github.com/YukiUmetsu/kumiki-blender/wiki/Install-on-Linux) (15–30 minutes).
3. [Make Your First 3D Model](https://github.com/YukiUmetsu/kumiki-blender/wiki/Make-Your-First-3D-Model): build a steel observation tower (10–30 minutes, depending on your computer).
4. [Open It in Blender](https://github.com/YukiUmetsu/kumiki-blender/wiki/Open-It-in-Blender): look around, zoom in, and save your work.
5. [Change Your First Model](https://github.com/YukiUmetsu/kumiki-blender/wiki/Change-Your-First-Model): make the tower a different color and height.

**Important:** Being able to export a model does not mean it is photorealistic or a faithful copy of a landmark. This project includes illustrative models, reusable components, and more advanced reconstruction tools. Quality still requires reference comparison and human artistic work.
